Common questions

I can't hear any sounds.

The game follows your iPhone or iPad's silent mode. If silent mode is on, the game plays no sounds but still vibrates. Turn silent mode off and turn the volume up to hear the beeps.

Is the game really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Does the game collect any data about my child?

No. It collects no personal data and uses no tracking or analytics. See our privacy policy.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game works fully offline.

Why does a link ask me a question?

Links to our website sit behind a short question for a grown-up (a "parental gate"), so children can't leave the game by accident.

How do I play?

Tap a fruit below the board to pick it up, then tap an empty cell to place it. Every row, every column and every box must hold each fruit exactly once. A mistake shows a sad face that fades after 30 seconds; five at once ends the game. The full rules are under "How to play / About us" in the game.
