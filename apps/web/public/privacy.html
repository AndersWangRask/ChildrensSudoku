Privacy Policy

Free Fruit Sudoku for Kids

Last updated: March 29, 2026

Overview

Free Fruit Sudoku for Kids is a children's puzzle game made by Famerlo. Your privacy is important to us, especially when it comes to children. This policy explains what data we collect — which is none.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No personal data is collected (no names, email addresses, or identifiers)
  • No usage analytics or tracking
  • No cookies
  • No location data
  • No camera or microphone access
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No in-app purchases

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools.

Children's Privacy

This app is designed for children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and similar regulations worldwide. Since we collect no data whatsoever, there is no risk to your child's privacy.

GDPR Compliance

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we confirm that:

  • No personal data is processed
  • No data is transferred to third parties or outside the EU
  • No consent is required because no data is collected
  • No data controller or processor appointment is necessary
  • The app meets the enhanced protections for children's data under GDPR Article 8

Offline Use

The app works entirely offline after installation. No internet connection is required to play, and no data is sent anywhere.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, we will post the changes on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
